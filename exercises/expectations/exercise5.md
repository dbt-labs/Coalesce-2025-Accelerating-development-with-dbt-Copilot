# Exercise 5 - what to expect

This exercise tests whether your standards generalize to a new business question, not just
the one you already fixed in Exercise 4.

**What's normal:** expect noticeably less correction needed than Exercise 1, since
`AGENTS.md` and the skill apply regardless of the specific business question they're used
for. It's still normal to catch one or two new things - a different question, built on
different columns, can surface a gap your standards didn't anticipate. That's expected,
ongoing refinement, not evidence that Exercise 3's work didn't hold up.

**The overall trend across all five exercises** is what matters here, more than any single
result: less correction needed each time a standard gets captured, even as the specific
business question changes.
