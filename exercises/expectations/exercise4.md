# Exercise 4 - what to expect

This is the payoff check for the standards you captured in Exercise 3. Expect a visibly
better result than Exercise 1 - not necessarily a perfect one.

**What's normal:** most tables see real improvement - fewer items from their Exercise 2
checklist come back wrong, because Wizard now has explicit instructions instead of only
what it can infer from surrounding files. It's also normal for one or two things to still be
off. That's useful signal that your `AGENTS.md` or skill needs to be more specific, not a
sign that anything failed.

**There's no pass/fail threshold.** The point of this exercise is the comparison against
Exercise 2, not hitting a perfect score. If something is still wrong, refining `AGENTS.md`
or the skill and trying again is exactly the intended next step, not a fallback for when
things go wrong.
